## At the edge [#en-el-borde]

- **All web traffic goes through Olimpia CDN.** Your apps get automatic HTTPS on `*.olimpia.cc` and on your custom domains, with attack protection at the edge. The region's servers only accept web traffic that comes from there.
- **Databases behind a proxy.** External access to Postgres and Redis goes through a TCP proxy with anti-DDoS protection and per-IP limits on open and new connections. TLS is required: unencrypted connections are dropped.
- **The region's address is not public.** No DNS record points directly to the servers where your resources run.

## Your secrets [#tus-secretos]

- **Encrypted environment variables.** Values are stored encrypted and are decrypted only to build and run your app.
- **Per-bucket credentials.** Each bucket has its own access key, with permissions only on that bucket. A leaked key can't be used to touch another one.
- **Rotatable credentials.** You can rotate your Postgres or Redis password, or a bucket's key, with one click. The old one stops working immediately.
- **Tokens we don't store.** For your sessions and tokens, we store only a hash. Revoking one invalidates it immediately.

## Your data [#tus-datos]

- **Postgres without superuser access for you or the explorer.** Your `app` user owns its database but is not a superuser. The console explorer connects with that same user, in read-only transactions.
- **Redis without admin commands.** The `default` user can't run `CONFIG`, `ACL`, `SHUTDOWN`, or other admin commands.
- **Backups under your control.** You schedule [Postgres backups](/postgres/backups) to an Olimpia bucket or to your own S3, with the frequency and retention you choose.
- **Resources capped per service.** Every app, database, and Redis instance runs with its own memory and CPU ceiling, so one service cannot starve the others.

## Your account [#tu-cuenta]

- Sign in with email and password or with GitHub.
- View and end your active sessions from the console.
- Sign-in attempts are rate-limited per IP and per account.
- **Account → Activity** shows what was done in your account from the console, the API, or your agents: what, when, and from where.
- Agents connect with [OAuth 2.1 and PKCE](/agentes/seguridad) or with personal tokens that you can revoke at any time.

## Report a problem [#reportar-un-problema]

If you find a vulnerability, email us at [hola@olimpia.dev](mailto:hola@olimpia.dev). We respond quickly and keep you updated on the fix.
