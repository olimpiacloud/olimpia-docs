Olimpia was born in Buenos Aires. The first region, **BUE**, runs in a datacenter in the city, on top-tier infrastructure. Your apps, your databases, and your Redis instances live there, side by side.

## Why it matters [#por-qué-importa]

- **Local latency.** If your users are in Argentina, your responses don't cross the continent to reach a server in Virginia or São Paulo. Your app and your database are in the same city as most of your users.
- **App and database, together.** Queries between your app and its database travel over the region's [internal network](/plataforma/red-interna), without going out to the internet.
- **Your data stays in the country.** Postgres databases and Redis instances store their data in Buenos Aires.

## How traffic gets in [#cómo-llega-el-tráfico]

```txt
navegador ── HTTPS ──▶ borde de Cloudflare ──▶ región BUE ──▶ tu app
cliente   ── TLS   ──▶ proxy TCP protegido ──▶ región BUE ──▶ tu base (si es pública)
```

- **Web and APIs.** All HTTP traffic comes in through the Cloudflare network, which terminates TLS close to the user and filters attacks before they reach the region. The origin only accepts connections that come from Cloudflare.
- **Databases with external access.** Connections to Postgres and Redis from outside Olimpia come in through a TCP proxy with DDoS protection, which forwards them over an encrypted tunnel. The region's address does not appear in any DNS record.
- **Builds.** Code is built on isolated machines, separate from where your apps run. A heavy build doesn't take CPU away from your production workloads.

## More regions [#más-regiones]

Within the region, each project runs on a server; the main one is `olimpia-bue1` ([Projects](/cuenta/proyectos#servidor)). Olimpia is designed to run in multiple regions. We'll announce new ones as they open. See [Coming soon](/proximamente/regiones).
