Team settings will let you work with other people in the same account. You'll be able to manage the team name, members, roles, and preferences.

## How it will work [#cómo-va-a-funcionar]

- You invite people by email.
- Each member has a role with its own permissions.
- Projects, apps, and databases belong to the team instead of to one person.

## In the meantime [#mientras-tanto]

Each account belongs to one person. To automate tasks without sharing your password, use [personal tokens](/cuenta/tokens). You can revoke each token separately.
