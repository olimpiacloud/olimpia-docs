Billing will show your monthly usage, your invoices, and your payment methods, in pesos or dollars.

## How billing will work [#cómo-se-va-a-cobrar]

Olimpia charges by usage: the CPU, memory, and storage of each resource. There are no capped plans and no artificial limits on the number of apps or databases. We will publish pricing before the region opens.

## In the meantime [#mientras-tanto]

No card is required during early access.
