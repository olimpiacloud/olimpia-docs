Stream will let you publish and consume real-time events between your services, in the same region as your apps.

## What it's for [#para-qué]

- **Payments and orders.** Publish an event when a payment comes in, and let each service react at its own pace.
- **Notifications.** Decouple sending emails, push notifications, and webhooks from the original request.
- **Logs and auditing.** Combine events from multiple apps into a single ordered stream.
- **Data pipelines.** Feed analytics processes without adding load to your main database.

## In the meantime [#mientras-tanto]

For job queues, use [Redis](/redis) with BullMQ, Sidekiq, or Celery. Redis never evicts keys to make room, so your queues do not lose queued jobs.
