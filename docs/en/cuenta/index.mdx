## Sign in [#entrar]

You can sign in to [olimpia.dev](https://olimpia.dev/login) in two ways:

- **Email and password.**
- **GitHub.** If your GitHub account has the same verified email as your Olimpia account, the two accounts are linked automatically.

The sign-in screen remembers which method you used last time.

:::note
During early access, Olimpia is invite-only. If your email or GitHub account doesn't have access yet, request it at [olimpia.dev](https://olimpia.dev).
:::

## Recover your password [#recuperar-la-contraseña]

On the sign-in screen, tap **Can't sign in?**, enter your email, and we'll send you a link. The link expires in one hour. Changing your password signs you out of all your other sessions.

## Devices [#dispositivos]

In **Devices**, from your account menu, you can see where you're signed in and when each session was last used. Sign out of any sessions you don't recognize, or sign out of all the others at once. Signing out of a session invalidates it immediately.

## Security limits [#límites-de-seguridad]

To stop brute-force attacks, sign-in, sign-up, and password recovery accept a limited number of attempts per minute from each IP address, and each account accepts a limited number of incorrect passwords every 15 minutes. If you reach the limit, wait a few minutes.

## Language and theme [#idioma-y-tema]

The console is available in Spanish and English, with light and dark themes.
