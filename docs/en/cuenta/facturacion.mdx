Olimpia charges for what your services really use: the memory, CPU, and disk they actually consume, measured every minute, not what they reserve. A stopped service costs nothing. Every plan includes usage, and prices are in US dollars, plus taxes.

## Plans [#planes]

| | Hobby | Pro |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Price** | USD 5 per month | USD 20 per month |
| **Included usage** | USD 5 per month | USD 20 per month |
| **Support** | By email | Priority, within one business day |

- Usage above what is included is paid by usage. On Hobby, if you use USD 3 you pay 5; if you use USD 8, you pay 8.
- Included usage does not roll over from one month to the next.
- Both plans have the same features.

## Usage prices [#precios-del-uso]

| Resource | Price |
| --- | --- |
| **Memory** | USD 10 per GB per month |
| **CPU** | USD 20 per vCPU per month |
| **Postgres disk** | USD 0.25 per GB per month |
| **Buckets** | USD 0.03 per GB per month |
| **Email** | USD 0.50 per 1,000 recipients |

- **Postgres and Redis are billed like an app**: their memory and CPU, and for Postgres also the disk the database takes up. There are no fixed database sizes.
- **Memory is measured without the file cache**, and CPU is what your services use, not what they have available.
- **Not billed**: builds, HTTPS certificates, custom domains, logs, metrics, and the MCP server.

An example: an app and a database that together average 0.5 GB of memory and 0.1 vCPU, with 2 GB in Postgres, add up to USD 5 + USD 2 + USD 0.50 = USD 7.50 of usage per month. On Hobby you pay USD 7.50; on Pro, USD 20.

## In the console [#en-la-consola]

**Account → Billing** shows:

- **Your plan**: what it costs, when it renews, and **Manage subscription and invoices**, which opens the payment portal to change your card, change or cancel the plan, and download your invoices. A canceled plan stays active until the end of the period.
- **Choose a plan**, if you don't have one yet: Hobby or Pro. It takes you to the payment page and brings you back to the console with the plan active.
- **Usage this period**: memory, CPU, disk, buckets, and emails sent, with what you used against what is included and the estimated amount to pay.
- **Usage by project**: what each project used in each month, at list price. Email is counted per account, not per project.

## During early access [#durante-el-acceso-anticipado]

Olimpia is invite-only and does not ask for a card to get started. Your usage is measured from day one and you can see it in **Billing**; charges start when you choose a plan.

## What is not available yet [#lo-que-todavía-no-está]

- **The free trial** shown on [olimpia.dev](https://olimpia.dev) (USD 5 of usage, once, for 30 days) is not active yet.
- **Spending caps** cannot be configured yet.
- **Outbound traffic** (USD 0.05 per GB) is not measured or billed yet.

## Limits [#límites]

The price depends on usage, but every service has a resource ceiling, the same on every plan:

| Service | Memory | CPU |
| --- | --- | --- |
| App | 4 GB | 4 vCPU |
| Postgres | 8 GB | 4 vCPU |
| Redis | 2 GB | 2 vCPU |

Each account can also have up to 20 apps.

## From your agent [#desde-tu-agente]

- `get_usage` returns the current usage of a project.
- `estimate_cost` simulates the monthly cost of a setup from its average usage, and what you would pay on Hobby and on Pro.

See [Tools](/agentes/herramientas#costos).
