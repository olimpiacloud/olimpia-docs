Olimpia is a cloud platform for running your product in production. Push your code, connect a database, and in a few minutes your app is online with HTTPS, running in Buenos Aires.

Everything lives inside **projects**: your apps, your Postgres databases, your Redis instances, and your buckets. You manage them from the [console](https://olimpia.dev/dashboard), or you ask your agent to do it for you over MCP.

## What you can run today [#qué-podés-correr-hoy]

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Apps" href="/apps" icon="rocket">
    Web services and APIs from GitHub, a Docker image, a local folder, or a template like n8n or cal.com. Automatic deploys on every push, with no downtime.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Postgres" href="/postgres" icon="database">
    Managed Postgres 18, with a data explorer, logs, metrics, and scheduled backups to your own S3.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Redis" href="/redis" icon="zap">
    Persistent Redis 8 for caching, sessions, and queues. It never evicts keys when memory runs low.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Buckets" href="/buckets" icon="archive">
    S3-compatible object storage, with separate credentials for each bucket. Your S3 SDKs work without changes.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

Every app also gets [Olimpia CDN](/cdn) and [Analytics](/apps/logs-y-metricas#analytics) with nothing to install, and your apps can send transactional [email](/email) from your own domain.

## Why Olimpia [#por-qué-olimpia]

- **Runs in Buenos Aires.** The BUE region is right here: your data stays close to your users in Argentina. [More about the region](/plataforma/buenos-aires).
- **Internal network.** Your apps reach their databases over a private network. Databases don't accept outside connections until you allow them. [How it works](/plataforma/red-interna).
- **CI/CD with GitHub.** Connect a repository, and every push to the branch you choose is built and published automatically, with no downtime and one-click rollback. [Deploy from GitHub](/apps/github).
- **Backups to your S3.** Schedule backups of your databases to AWS S3, Cloudflare R2, Backblaze B2, MinIO, or any compatible service. [Set up backups](/postgres/backups).
- **Built for agents.** Olimpia has an MCP server with 37 tools. Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, and any MCP client can deploy, query, and debug for you. [For agents](/agentes).
- **No code changes.** Postgres and Redis work the way they always have, and buckets speak the S3 API. Your drivers, ORMs, and SDKs work the same.
