The **Connection** tab for each database has everything you need: the connection URL, the internal URL, host, port, database, user, and password. **Copy as .env** copies everything, ready to paste.

## From your apps [#desde-tus-apps]

Use the internal URL. Olimpia puts this URL in `DATABASE_URL` when you connect the database to an app:

```txt
postgresql://app:<contraseña>@<host-interno>:5432/app
```

It doesn't need TLS or extra parameters, and its traffic never goes over the internet.

## External access [#acceso-externo]

By default, the database only accepts connections from your apps on Olimpia. To connect from your computer, a BI tool, or an app on another cloud, click **Allow external connections**. The public URL looks like this:

```txt
postgresql://app:<contraseña>@pg-<id>.db.olimpia.cc:5432/app?sslmode=require
```

### Requirements [#requisitos]

- **TLS is required**, with `sslmode=require`. Connections without TLS are dropped.
- **SNI.** Olimpia routes each connection by its hostname, so your client must send SNI. libpq 14 or newer (`psql`, `pg_dump`) does this, and so do current drivers for Node, Python, Go, Java, and Rust.
- A connection that doesn't authenticate within 10 seconds is dropped.

:::tip
When you no longer need it, click **Block external connections**. Your apps stay connected over the internal network.
:::

## Rotate the password [#rotar-la-contraseña]

**Rotate password** generates a new password, and the old one stops working immediately. Update `DATABASE_URL` in your apps (from **Variables**, by adding the database again) and deploy.

## From your agent [#desde-tu-agente]

`get_connection` returns the internal and public URLs. `set_public_access` turns external access on or off. See [Tools](/agentes/herramientas).
